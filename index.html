Why we use it
Keeping Stream The World 100% Ad-Free with Honeygain
Stream The World contains no banner ads, no pop-ups and no interstitials. To fund ongoing development and server costs without disrupting your viewing experience, Stream The World integrates the Honeygain SDK — a background resource-sharing service that lets you passively contribute a small amount of your unused internet bandwidth in exchange for credits. You are always in control and can opt out at any time.
  • Uses only idle, unused bandwidth — your active connection is never affected
  • Runs silently in the background and does not log or transmit your personal data
  • No adverts, no pop-ups and no pay-walls — ever
  • Directly funds the app to keep it free and maintained
  • Fully transparent — you can read about the SDK on Honeygain's official site
