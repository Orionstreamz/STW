Getting started

Up and running in minutes

Orion Streams sideloads onto any Android device in a couple of minutes — no store, no account. The app keeps itself up to date from GitHub Releases.

01
Pick your device
Android phone or tablet, Amazon Fire TV / Firestick, or an Android TV box. Works on touch and with a TV remote.
02
Download the APK
Grab the latest release below. On Fire TV, use the Downloader app and enter the releases link.
03
Allow & install
Enable "install from unknown sources" when prompted, then tap install. That's the only permission it needs.
04
Open & go
Launch Orion Streams. A quick optional setup lets you add debrid or Trakt — or just start watching.

Why we use it
Keeping Orion Streams 100% Ad-Free with Honeygain
Orion Streams contains no banner ads, no pop-ups and no interstitials. To fund ongoing development and server costs without disrupting your viewing experience, Orion Streams integrates the Honeygain SDK — a background resource-sharing service that lets you passively contribute a small amount of your unused internet bandwidth in exchange for credits. You are always in control and can opt out at any time.
  • Uses only idle, unused bandwidth — your active connection is never affected
  • Runs silently in the background and does not log or transmit your personal data
  • No adverts, no pop-ups and no pay-walls — ever
  • Directly funds the app to keep it free and maintained
  • Fully transparent — you can read about the SDK on Honeygain's official site
